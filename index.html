Three RoboCup@Home teams.
One joint team.

tRES brings together Re@dy (National Institute of Technology, Kitakyushu College), eR@sers (Tamagawa University) and SOBITS (Soka University) to build service robots that help people at home. We are preparing for RoboCup@Home 2027 with a Unitree G1 humanoid and a Toyota HSR.

“Tres” means “three” in Spanish, and R, E and S stand for Re@dy, eR@sers and SOBITS.

Purpose

Why a joint team?

Homes are complex places: robots need to move safely, understand what people ask, find and handle everyday objects, and work alongside people. No single lab covers all of this. tRES combines complementary strengths so that each idea can be tested on real robots in a real competition.

Humanoid robots at home

Bringing a full-size humanoid (Unitree G1) into domestic tasks such as serving and interacting with people.

Robots that understand homes

Concept models of places and people, fine-tuned Vision-Language-Action models, and simulation copies of real arenas.

Sharing with the community

Open-source software, simulation tools and documentation that other @Home teams can reuse.

Research topics for 2027 are candidates and may change. Read more about our research.
