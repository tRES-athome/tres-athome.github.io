Other robots in the partner labs

The partner teams also develop and use other robots for research and education. These are not planned competition platforms for 2027.

SOBITS robots

In-house robots designed with 3D CAD and 3D printing: SOBIT EDU and SOBIT MINI (based on TurtleBot2), SOBIT PRO (four-wheel independent steering), SOBIT LIGHT (open-source mobile manipulator on a Kachaka base) and SOBIT HOME (dual-arm mobile manipulator).

SOBITS robots

eR@sers robots

Besides the HSR and G1: Kachaka (Preferred Robotics) as an education platform, two PiPER arms (AgileX) for dual-arm and VLA research, and a UR5e arm (Universal Robots) for tabletop tasks.
